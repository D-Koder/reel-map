Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Waylit Maps helps you save places from reels, vote on them with the people you plan with, and turn them into plans. This page explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • We collect what you give us (account, places, votes, bookings, reviews, photos) and a little technical data to run the app.
  • People in a collection you share can see what is in that collection.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.
  • Waylit Maps is for people aged 13 and over.
  • You can delete your account and your data yourself in the app, or by emailing us.

Who we are

Waylit Maps ("we", "us"). Contact: hello@waylitmaps.com. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles, and we respect the privacy rights of people in other countries too.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider), display name, avatar (a preset or a photo you upload), and optionally your country.
  • Your content: collections, places you save, reel links, notes, your votes, bookings and plans (including times and who made them), event details, reviews and the photos you attach, and booking confirmation files you upload.
  • Waitlist: if you joined our waitlist, your email address.
  • Messages to us: anything you send to hello@waylitmaps.com.

Information from your device and use of the app

  • Time zone, so times in notifications and your weekly pick match where you are.
  • Push notification details (a code that lets us send a notification to your device), if you turn notifications on.
  • Location: only if you switch on "my location" or nearby alerts. It is used on your device. We do not save it. If location is on, your coordinates may be sent to our map search provider to rank nearby results.
  • Google Calendar (optional): if you connect it, we read only whether you are free or busy, in your browser, to help pick times. We do not store your calendar. If you choose to add a plan to your calendar, we create that event.
  • iPhone Shortcut key: a private key that lets your Shortcut save reels to your account.
  • Error and technical data: if something breaks, an error report (what went wrong, the page, your browser and device type) may be sent to our error-monitoring service. Our servers also see your IP address in standard request logs.

Information we look up

When you save a reel, we read the public reel page (link, caption, tagged place) and use it to work out which place it is. We look the place up on Google Maps to get its address, hours, phone number and website. These results are saved and reused, for up to 100 days, so we do not look up the same thing twice.

How we use it

  • To run Waylit Maps: save and show your places, let you share and vote, make and remind you about plans.
  • To send notifications you ask for (reminders, votes, new places, booking changes, your weekly pick). You can change these in Settings.
  • To keep the service safe, fix problems, and stop abuse.
  • To email you about your account, or about launching if you joined the waitlist.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who can see your information

  • People in your collections can see your display name, avatar, the places in the collection, your votes, plans and notes.
  • Reviews are public inside the app. A review you write about a place, with any photos you attach and your display name and avatar, can be seen by every signed-in Waylit Maps user, not only the people in your collections. You can edit or remove your reviews at any time.
  • Our service providers (below), who handle data for us.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

Cookies and device storage

We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in and to remember settings on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them.
  • Booking confirmation files: removed automatically soon after a plan ends, or when you remove them.
  • Saved place and reel lookups: up to 100 days.
  • Waitlist email: until we launch and email you, or you ask us to remove it.
  • Backups: deleted data may stay in our provider's backups for a limited time before it is overwritten.

Deleting your data

In the app

Open Settings, go to your profile, and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, your votes, plans, reviews, notifications, push details and the photos and files you uploaded.

Important: collections you own are deleted too, and other members lose them. To keep a collection, give it to another member before you delete. Places you added to other people's collections are removed as well.

By email

Email hello@waylitmaps.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

Waitlist only

Email us and we will remove your address.

Children

Waylit Maps is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, and you must be 13 or older to create an account. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has an account, email hello@waylitmaps.com with the child's account email address. We will delete the account and its data promptly, and within 30 days at the latest. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 13, we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can change most of it yourself in the app. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have the right to object to or restrict some processing and to receive your data in a portable form. Email us and we will help.

If you are not happy with how we handled a privacy matter, tell us first. In Australia you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database limits each person to the data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell the people affected and the regulator as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app or by email.
