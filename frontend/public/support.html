Saving places

How do I save a reel?

Three ways:

  • Paste: copy the reel's link, open Waylit Maps and tap Paste a reel link.
  • Share (Android): in Instagram or TikTok tap Share, then choose Waylit Maps. Install the app to your home screen first.
  • iPhone Shortcut: saves a reel without leaving Instagram. Set it up in Settings, under "Save reels without leaving Instagram".
It found the wrong place

When you paste a reel you are asked to confirm the place. Tap Wrong place and pick the right one, or search Google Maps yourself. In the Add a place screen you can tap Report problem and tell us what went wrong.

The reel lists several places

You will see a list. Tick the places you want, then tap Save selected.

My Shortcut stopped working

Open Settings, then the Shortcut section, and tap Reconnect. If that does not work, tap Make a new key and then Connect again.

Sharing and plans

How do I invite my partner or friends?

Open a collection's Share screen and tap Send invite to my partner. The link and code work for 7 days. The other person opens the link, or enters the code in Settings under "Join a collection".

How do bookings and reminders work?

Pick a time for a place and everyone in that collection is told. You get reminders as the time gets close. A booking in Waylit Maps is a plan for your group. It does not reserve a table. Use the place's own booking link or phone number for that.

What is the weekly pick?

Once a week we suggest one saved place you have not planned yet, at 7pm your time. Choose the day, or turn it off, in Settings under Weekly pick.

Notifications

I am not getting notifications
  • iPhone: add Waylit Maps to your Home Screen first (Share, then Add to Home Screen), open it from there, and allow notifications.
  • Check Settings, Notifications is switched on in the app.
  • Check your phone's own settings are not blocking Waylit Maps.

Your account and data

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap the forgot password link and we will email you a reset link. Check your spam folder.

How do I download my places?

Open a collection's Share screen and choose the Waylit Maps file (or spreadsheet) download.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings, go to your profile, and tap Delete account. If you own collections, give them to another member first if you want to keep them, because they are deleted with your account. You can also email us from your account address and we will do it for you. See the Privacy Policy for what is deleted.

My child has an account

Waylit Maps is for people 13 and over. Email us the child's account address and we will delete the account.

Safety

How do I report a review or someone's behaviour?

Tap the report option on a review. For anything else, email us with details and we will look into it.

I think my account was accessed by someone else

Change your password straight away, then email us.
