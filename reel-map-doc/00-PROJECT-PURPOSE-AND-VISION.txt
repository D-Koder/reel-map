===============================================
REEL MAP - PURPOSE, VISION & VALUES
===============================================


===============================================
WHY THIS PROJECT EXISTS
===============================================

THE PROBLEM (Real):
  Every week, your partner/friend sends you Instagram Reels of cafes, restaurants,
  events they think you'd like. You screenshot them. Bookmark them. Pin them.
  Then you forget them.
  
  By Friday, you've lost track of 20+ places.
  By next week, you've forgotten them entirely.
  
  Result: Great locations go unexplored. Date ideas disappear. Memories never happen.


THE ROOT CAUSE:
  Instagram Reels are BEAUTIFUL but EPHEMERAL.
  They're designed for quick scroll → forget.
  They're not designed for ACTION.
  
  There's no "save this for later" workflow that LEADS TO BOOKING.


THE INSIGHT:
  People don't need another bookmark app.
  People need a PLANNING TOOL that turns Reels into REAL DATES.
  
  Not: "save this place"
  But: "save this place → rate it together → book it → go → remember it"


===============================================
PROJECT PURPOSE (The Why)
===============================================

TO SOLVE:
  Turn shared Instagram Reels → booked reservations → completed adventures

FOR:
  Couples and friend groups who want to explore more, together

SO THAT:
  They don't lose amazing ideas
  They plan more dates confidently
  They create better shared memories
  They stop wasting great suggestions


PRIMARY PURPOSE:
  "Reel Map is the missing link between Instagram discovery and real-world action.
   We turn fleeting Reel moments into planned adventures couples will remember."


SECONDARY PURPOSES:
  • Reduce decision fatigue (curated by each other, not algorithms)
  • Strengthen relationships (shared planning ritual)
  • Fight FOMO (follow through on ideas instead of scrolling past them)
  • Create accountability (both users committed to the plan)


===============================================
VISION (3-5 year outcome)
===============================================

5-YEAR VISION:
  "Reel Map becomes the go-to way for couples and friends to discover,
   plan, and document shared adventures. It's how people turn casual
   Instagram scrolling into real, memorable experiences together."

WHEN SUCCESSFUL:
  • Every couple we know uses it
  • "Let me save that to Reel Map" becomes a natural phrase
  • Users can look back on their year and see: 50 places saved, 20 visited

===============================================
CORE BELIEFS
===============================================

BELIEF 1: Shared Curation > Algorithmic Feed
  We believe people discover better places when curating FOR EACH OTHER,
  not when algorithms decide what to show.
  
  Instagram shows you what gets engagement.
  Your partner shows you what they think YOU'LL love.
  
  ACTION: Make it easy for partners to share. Celebrate shared ratings,
  not individual scroll time.


BELIEF 2: Planning IS the Experience
  We believe the act of planning together is part of the adventure.
  
  It's not just about the place—it's about the conversation.
  "Should we try this?" "When?" "Yes, let's book it!" → connection.
  
  ACTION: Make the planning ritual enjoyable. Progressive steps.
  Celebrate booked dates, not just saved ones.


BELIEF 3: Completion Matters More Than Saving
  We believe quantity of saved locations is meaningless.
  
  100 saved locations you never visit = guilt.
  5 completed adventures = memories forever.
  
  ACTION: Default to "Do Soon" (5-item limit). Track completion.
  Celebrate streak, not collection size.


BELIEF 4: Intimacy Over Scale
  We believe this is not a social network for strangers.
  
  This is a tool for couples and close friends to coordinate better.
  No public profiles. No liking other people's collections.
  No feed algorithm. No influencer culture.
  
  ACTION: Private by default. Invite only. No follower counts.


BELIEF 5: Friction Prevents Action
  We believe most Reel tools fail because there are too many steps
  between discovery and booking.
  
  Copy link → paste → search → save → later...
  
  By the time you get there, you've forgotten why it mattered.
  
  ACTION: 2-click saves. One modal for entire booking flow.
  No separate "booking" app—do it here.


BELIEF 6: Accountability Drives Completion
  We believe having a partner committed to the same plan increases
  the chance you'll actually go.
  
  Booking alone = easy to cancel.
  Booking with your partner = harder to back out.
  
  ACTION: Show partner's commitment. Joint checklist.
  Streak rewards (both of you).


BELIEF 7: Transparency Builds Trust
  We believe sharing should be intentional, not creepy.
  
  No tracking partners without consent.
  No selling data.
  No ads.
  
  ACTION: Ask permission to share. Show what data is stored.
  Privacy-first settings.


===============================================
CORE VALUES
===============================================

1. INTENTIONALITY
   We believe everything should serve a purpose.
   No feature bloat. No dark patterns. No engagement metrics above user good.
   
   • Every button should move users toward booking
   • Every notification should prompt action, not just engagement
   • Every design choice should ask: does this help plan a date?

2. SIMPLICITY
   We believe less is more.
   Power users want 100 features. Real couples want 5 that work.
   
   • One collection type (not 5)
   • One rating system (😐/🤔/🔥)
   • One booking flow (not 3 ways)

3. TRANSPARENCY
   We believe users deserve to know what we're doing with their data.
   No hidden algorithms. No surprise features. No "free" with strings.
   
   • Clear privacy policy
   • Show where data goes
   • No selling data ever
   • Honest about limitations

4. JOY
   We believe planning should be fun, not another task.
   Gradients, emojis, celebrations, streaks.
   
   • Celebrate milestones ("🔥 2 adventures this month")
   • Use delightful language
   • Make rating together feel like play, not work
   • Green dots that pulse when they see their booking is live

5. RELIABILITY
   We believe broken promises kill relationships.
   If we say we'll save your data, we do.
   If we say we'll email you an invite, it arrives.
   
   • No downtime without warning
   • Data backups daily
   • Support emails answered same day
   • Uptime SLA: 99.5%


===============================================
PROBLEM WE'RE NOT SOLVING
===============================================

❌ GROUP TRIPS (3+ people)
   That's a different problem. Different UX. Different booking flow.
   We're starting with couples, then expanding.

❌ SOLO TRAVEL
   Reel Map is intentionally social. Solo users welcome,
   but the value is in shared planning.

❌ RESERVATION SYSTEM REPLACEMENT
   We're not building OpenTable 2.0.
   We're LINKING to existing reservation systems, not replacing them.

❌ SOCIAL NETWORK
   This is not Instagram for food lovers.
   This is a planning tool, not a sharing/bragging platform.

❌ MONETIZATION FIRST
   We're not optimizing for ads or premium paywalls.
   We're optimizing for real dates happening.
   Monetization comes later (if at all).


===============================================
HOW WE MEASURE SUCCESS
===============================================

METRIC 1: Booking Completion
  "Did users actually book?"
  • Target: 60% of 🔥🔥 items → booked reservation
  • This matters more than: # reels saved, # users, # invites sent

METRIC 2: Adventure Completion  
  "Did they actually go?"
  • Target: 50% of booked items → visited + marked done
  • This matters more than: # bookings made, # reviews written

METRIC 3: Shared Planning
  "Are partners using it together?"
  • Target: 80% of collections have 2+ raters
  • This matters more than: # solo collections, # downloads

METRIC 4: Retention
  "Are couples coming back?"
  • Target: 60% 30-day retention
  • This matters more than: # signups, # viral coefficient

METRIC 5: Joy (Qualitative)
  "Do users smile when using this?"
  • Survey: "Rate your experience" (target: 4.5+/5)
  • Open feedback: "Best date planning tool we've used"
  • NPS: 50+


===============================================
NOT WHAT WE MEASURE
===============================================

❌ Daily Active Users
   Who cares if they open it daily? We care if they plan well weekly.

❌ Viral Coefficient
   We don't want viral. We want sticky.
   Couples telling one other couple is fine. We're not TikTok.

❌ Average Session Length
   Short sessions are good (plan → close → book).
   Long sessions might mean we're too complicated.

❌ Click-through Rates
   We're not optimizing for engagement metrics.
   We're optimizing for real-world outcomes.

❌ Growth Rate
   Steady, sustainable growth beats hockey-stick hockey stick.
   We'd rather have 1,000 couples who love it
   than 100,000 who tried once.


===============================================
OUR PHILOSOPHY ON DESIGN
===============================================

PHILOSOPHY: "Decisions are visible, not friction."

What this means:
  Users should see why they're making a choice, not be annoyed by it.
  
  Example (What NOT to do):
    "You must verify your email to continue" ← friction
  
  Example (What we DO):
    "Sarah needs to rate this too before we can decide.
     (1/2 ratings)" ← transparency


DESIGN PRINCIPLES:

1. PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE
   Show step 1. Complete it. Show step 2.
   Don't show all 3 booking steps at once.

2. CELEBRATE PROGRESS
   "✓ Step 1 complete" → dopamine hit

3. CLEAR NEXT STEPS
   Never leave user wondering "what now?"
   Every screen has 1 primary action.

4. REDUCE DECISION PARALYSIS
   "Do Soon" = 5 items max (not 50)
   Collections = user-created (not algorithm-suggested)
   Ratings = 3 options (not 10-star scale)

5. MAKE BREAKING UP EASY
   Want to unfriend your partner? One button.
   Want to delete a collection? Confirm, done.
   Want to leave the app? We won't beg you.
